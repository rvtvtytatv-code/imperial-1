POKER RULES & HAND RANKINGS

Poker is a family of card games. Texas Hold’em is one of the best-known variants. This guide explains the rules and terminology, rather than the current game schedule of any club.

How to play Texas Hold’em

Private cards

Each player receives two private cards. The small blind and big blind are compulsory bets that begin a hand in standard Hold’em.

Preflop and flop

The first betting round takes place before the community cards are dealt. The flop consists of three shared cards, followed by another betting round.

Turn and river

A fourth shared card (the turn) and then a fifth (the river) are dealt, with betting rounds after each.

Showdown

If more than one player remains, hands are compared. Each player makes the best five-card hand from their two private cards and the five community cards. If all other players fold, the remaining player wins the pot without a showdown.

Common poker terms

Fold
Give up the current hand.
Check
Pass the action without betting when no bet is outstanding.
Call
Match the outstanding bet.
Raise
Increase the current bet.
Pot
The total chips committed to the hand.
