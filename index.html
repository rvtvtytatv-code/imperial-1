Poker rules and hand rankings

Where do I play?
IMPERIVM operates on ClubGG Poker, available for Android, iPhone and PC.
What is the IMPERIVM Club ID?
Our Club ID is 840247. Enter it in ClubGG or use the direct club invitation on this page.
How do I get approved?
Message @IMPERIVM_Support with your ClubGG username and the name of the club you joined. The team will guide you through approval.
How can I contact the team?
Use our Telegram bot or message @IMPERIVM_Support directly. Personal player support is available 24/7.
How do I find a poker club on ClubGG?
Open the Club section in ClubGG Poker, enter the club ID or follow the invitation link. IMPERIVM uses club ID 840247.
Can I play online poker on my phone?
ClubGG Poker is available on Android, iPhone and PC. Download the appropriate version for your device and check the local rules before accessing the service.
What is Texas Hold’em poker?
Texas Hold’em is a poker variant in which each player receives two private cards and can use five shared cards to form the best five-card hand.
